How to Play

Pick your Hashima, then battle! Use Smash Bros-style controls:

Player 1 (WASD)

Movement: A/D to move, W to jump

Attacks: Space (Neutral), Shift (Side), Ctrl (Up), Alt (Down)

Defense: Q to shield, E to roll, S to fast fall

Player 2 (Arrow Keys)

Movement: ←/→ to move, ↑ to jump

Attacks: Enter (Neutral), R (Side), T (Up), Y (Down)

Defense: F to shield, G to roll, ↓ to fast fall
